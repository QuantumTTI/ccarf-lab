Enter QUIZ mode (see CLAUDE.md). Scope: $ARGUMENTS (default: "weak" — the
weakest areas recorded in PROGRESS.md).

Standard drill (a domain, statement, or "weak"):
- 10 original questions, one at a time. Realistic production stem anchored in
  one of the six official scenarios; four options, ALL plausible; wrong
  answers are disproportionate (over-engineered, mis-sized, wrong root cause),
  not silly. Include at least 2 multiple-response items and state how many to
  select.
- Grade immediately after each answer: correct letter(s), then a rationale
  that names WHY each distractor fails, referencing the five judgment
  templates and the trap index in CURRICULUM.md Part C.

"mock" (full exam simulation):
- Pick 4 of the 6 official scenarios at random. 60 questions total,
  distributed by blueprint weight (D1 27%, D2 18%, D3 20%, D4 20%, D5 15%),
  grouped under their scenario like the real exam. Encourage a 120-minute
  timer. Grade at the END only: per-domain percentage table, overall
  percentage, a rough scaled-score estimate against the 720/1000 pass mark,
  and the three weakest topics with recommended /teach or /practice reruns.

Rules:
- Never reuse a question verbatim from CURRICULUM.md, the official exam guide,
  or a previous quiz in PROGRESS.md — always write original items in the same
  style.
- Update PROGRESS.md: quiz date, scope, score per domain; flag any domain
  under 85% as a weak area.
